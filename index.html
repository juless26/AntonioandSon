About Antonio & Son

Antonio Ibarra and his son have designed and cared for gardens across Silicon Valley since 2020.

They build Mediterranean gardens with plants that suit the local climate, such as olive trees, lavender, rosemary and succulents. These plants need little water once they take root, and they look good all year.

Antonio works with his son and a crew of two, from the first plan to the regular visits.

Services

Landscape design

  • New lawns and sod
  • Flower beds, shrubs and trees
