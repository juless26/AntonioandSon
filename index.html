About Antonio & Son

Antonio Ibarra and his son have designed and cared for gardens across Silicon Valley since 2020.

They build Mediterranean gardens with plants that suit the local climate, such as olive trees, lavender, rosemary and succulents. These plants need little water once they settle in, and they look good all year.

Antonio works with his son and a crew of two, from the first plan to the regular visits.

Services

Landscape design

For a new yard, or a yard you want to change.

  • Planting plans for front and back yards
  • New lawns and sod
  • Flower beds, shrubs and trees
  • New pathways and gravel
  • Sprinkler and drip irrigation installation

Maintenance

Regular visits to keep the yard healthy and tidy.

  • Mowing and edging
  • Hedge and shrub trimming
  • Weeding and cleanup
  • Fertilizing and seasonal planting
  • Sprinkler checks and repairs
